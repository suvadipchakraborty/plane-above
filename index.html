How to play

 
Every day there is one flight. You see where it left from, who flies it and on what aircraft. The destination is hidden. Name the airport in 5 guesses.

 
Reading your guess

 
XYZCity
4,210 km

The distance is measured in a straight line from your guess to the real destination. The arrow shows which way to fly from your guess to reach it.

 
  • Correct airport
  • Within 1,500 km
  • Farther than 1,500 km

 
Good to know

 
    
  
  • A new flight appears at 00:00 UTC, the same for every player.
    • 
  
  • Flight time is estimated from distance. Real schedules vary.
    • 
  
  • Win in a row to build a streak. Your stats stay on this device.
    • 
  
  • Finish a puzzle to copy an emoji grid you can share.
